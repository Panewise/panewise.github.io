Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version

Panewise extensions collect nothing, send nothing, and store nothing about you on any server.

There is no account to create, no analytics, no tracking pixel, and no telemetry. The rest of this page is the same statement in detail, because a store review needs the detail.

Gridpull

Gridpull reads the table on the page you are looking at and turns it into a file. All of that happens inside your browser.

Page content
Read only while you have the extension open on that tab, and only to build your export. It is never transmitted, never stored, and is gone when the popup closes.
Where the file goes
To your own downloads folder or your clipboard. It does not pass through any server.
Network requests
None. Gridpull works with the internet disconnected.
Licence key
Stored locally in your browser so Pro stays unlocked. It is not linked to your identity and is not sent anywhere.
Which tabs
Only the tab where you click the toolbar button. It does not run in the background and cannot read other tabs.

Permissions, and why each one exists

activeTab
Grants access to the current tab at the moment you click the button, and nothing beyond it.
scripting
Places the table reader into that tab when you ask for an export.
storage
Holds the licence key locally. Nothing else is written.

There is deliberately no broad host permission. An extension that can read every site you visit is not something you should have to trust, so Gridpull does not ask for it.

Payments

Pro licences are sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as merchant of record and handles the transaction and any applicable tax. Your payment details go to them and are never seen by Panewise. Their privacy policy covers that part of the process.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what an extension does will ship alongside a new version in the store.
