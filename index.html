Browser extensions that take the repetition out of tools your team already uses all day.

What we build

One task, done properly

Narrow tools rather than suites. Each extension removes a single recurring action — nothing to configure, no dashboard to learn.

Made for work, not browsing

Built for people who repeat the same handful of clicks dozens of times a day because it is their job: operations, support, marketing, online sellers.

Free until it earns its keep

Every extension has a free tier with a usage limit that genuinely covers light use. The paid plan lifts the limit. Monthly, cancel whenever.

How it is built

Platform
Chrome and Edge, on Manifest V3.
Permissions
An extension requests access only to the sites it actually works on — never all URLs for convenience.
Page data
What you see in your browser stays in your browser. We do not collect page content.
Billing
Subscriptions are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record, which covers VAT and sales tax wherever you are.
