Panewise · Gridpull

Product demo

Fifty-six seconds, captioned: a 237-row report that the page shows fifty rows at a time, exported into one file.

What happens in the recording

  1. 0:05

    A report of 237 invoices. The page shows 50 of them and hides the rest behind a pager — the ordinary shape of an internal business report.

  2. 0:10

    Page 1 of 5. Without a tool, this is where someone starts copying into Excel five separate times.

  3. 0:17

    Gridpull scans the page, finds the table, and flags that more pages sit behind it.

  4. 0:27

    On the free plan the export takes this page only — 50 rows — and says so rather than quietly shortening the file.

  5. 0:35

    A licence key unlocks Pro. It is kept in the browser and never sent anywhere.

  6. 0:43

    The multi-page option becomes available: walk through every page and merge.

  7. 0:49

    Gridpull presses the page’s own Next button five times, merges what it finds, and reports: 237 rows from 5 pages.

Worth knowing

Nothing in this recording is mocked up. It is the extension’s real interface reading a real table, and the row count at the end is the actual number of rows collected. The page used is our public test report, which anyone can open.

Gridpull makes no network requests. The whole process runs inside the browser, and works with the machine offline.
